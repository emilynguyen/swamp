# Color palette

Source of truth: Figma variable collection `Colors`.

## Primary

**Yellow** represents growth, optimism, and the light we help bring to businesses navigating a crowded digital landscape. It's warm and approachable rather than corporate, representing our friendly, non-serious brand positioning.

  <ColorItem title="Brand" subtitle="primary-brand" colors={{ Brand: '#FCF47A' }} />
</ColorPalette>

## Secondary

**Brown** is grounded and earthy — it represents the roots, the foundational strategy and craft work that sits beneath every project. It balances the yellow's energy with something organic and trustworthy.

**Purple** adds depth and a touch of the unexpected, the layered, saturated undergrowth of the swamp ecosystem. It represents the creative, expressive side of our work and signals digital/innovative thinking without losing warmth.

## Neutrals

A soft, misty light neutral used for backgrounds and breathing room. It keeps layouts calm and legible, letting the primary and secondary colors do the talking. Paired with a deep, swamp-water tone rather than true black — softer, warmer, and more organic.
